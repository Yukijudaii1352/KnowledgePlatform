Intended Usage and Limitations
Benefit and Intended Usage
Gemini Robotics-ER 1.6 represents an advancement to our reasoning-first Gemini Robotics-ER models that enables robots to understand their environments with precision. This model specializes in reasoning critical for robotics, including visual and spatial understanding, task planning and success detection.
